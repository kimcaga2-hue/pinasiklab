About EcoBaguio

EcoBaguio is a community-based digital platform designed to improve waste management and promote environmental awareness in Baguio City. It provides residents with an accessible way to report improper waste disposal, overflowing garbage bins, illegal dumping, and other environmental concerns within their communities.

Through its reporting system, residents can submit waste-related complaints, provide descriptions, and pinpoint the exact location of reported problems using an interactive map. This helps make environmental concerns easier to identify, monitor, and address.

EcoBaguio also promotes proper waste segregation, responsible waste disposal, and active community participation. By connecting residents with a centralized reporting and monitoring platform, the project aims to encourage cooperation between communities and local waste management authorities.

Our goal is to help create a cleaner, greener, and healthier Baguio City through technology, community involvement, and environmental responsibility.
